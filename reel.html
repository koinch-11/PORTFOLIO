ONE DAY HIKE


Jamacho Gumba


Trip Overview

One of the shortest destinations from Kathmandu- Hiking to Jamacho Hill is a less crowded jungle walk inside the National Park. Walking via the bushes,you can see the wildlife, natural beauty, views of the mesmerizing mountains, different kinds of birds breathing the fresh air. Elevating at 2130m above sea level, Jamacho hill is a Buddhist shrine. You can see a stupa, a small monastery there. Climbing the watchtower offers you panoramic 360-degree views of the landscapes, mountains, and Kathmandu valley. Chiliads of prayer flags are hung around the stupa and monastery which have added the beauty of the peak. Reaching Jamacho hill one feels peaceful seeing the beauty of the surroundings. Being part of the Shivapuri- Nagarjuna National park, your journey begins after a short drive to Phulbari Gate, (near Balaju Bypass). Entering the jungle, you will be escaped from the hassle of Kathmandu Valley. After 2:30 hours’ upward hiking, you reach Jamacho Peak. Capturing there around, having the picturesque views of the hills, lush valley, and snow-clad peaks, you hike back following the same route. As you hike downward, you can reach the bottom within 2 hours. Catching the car from the gate, we get back to your hotel. For those travelers who seek a short and easy walk on the hill, this hike can be the best alternatives. Visitors having a short time in Kathmandu can afford this journey easily as it finishes within 6-7 hours.

Is this Hike for You?

Jamacho Peak Hike is an easy graded hike that completes within 6-7 hours. As this place is quite near to Kathmandu, anybody having a short time in Kathmandu can afford this hike. You need not do any extra exercises and preparations before this trek. Travelers having moderate physical fitness can complete this journey easily. What kind of things are prohibited in the Hike? As you are hiking inside the National Park, some instruments are not allowed to take on the trip. You cannot carry speakers, knives, matchboxes, lighters, or any kinds of instruments that produce fire. You are not allowed to make noise or disturb the inhabitants there. But you can carry the packed lunch, water, and other soft drinks easily. Note: As there are no teahouses or lunch places on the way, hikers should bring water, drinks, and some packed food from Kathmandu. At the top, you can buy some drinks and junk food but they are many expensive ones.
